### App Review notes
Accessible Backrooms is an audio horror game designed for blind and low-vision players. The whole game can be played with VoiceOver, with the game's own narration, or by voice commands; there is also a visual 3D view and pictures for sighted players.

How to test: open the app, choose a language, then "Enter, with narration" (or "Enter, I use a screen reader" if VoiceOver is on). Press Play, then Start game. A short tutorial starts the first time. Use the on-screen buttons (Go forward, Listen, Turn left) or say the commands out loud after allowing the microphone. Headphones are recommended.

The microphone and speech recognition are only used for voice commands, are optional, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. No account or login is needed. No in-app purchases.
