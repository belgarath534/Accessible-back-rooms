Accessible Backrooms Uploader

1. Unzip this folder on your Windows computer.
2. Copy your key file into this folder. It is the file you downloaded from Apple, named AuthKey_ followed by letters, ending in .p8.
3. Double click "Upload screenshots.bat".
4. When it asks, paste your Issuer ID and press Enter.
5. Wait until it says ALL DONE, then refresh App Store Connect.

Your key stays on your computer. It is only sent to Apple, to sign in.
You can run it again safely: it replaces the screenshots instead of adding copies.
